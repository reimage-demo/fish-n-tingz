Accessibility

Updated October 11, 2026

Getting assistance

If you have difficulty using this website, call (860) 730-4440. The restaurant can help with menu information, reservation requests, and questions about visiting the space.

When reporting a website problem, please describe the page and the action you were trying to complete. Sharing your browser or assistive technology, if you wish, can help identify the issue.

Website features

The website includes keyboard-operable navigation and buttons, visible focus indicators, labeled form fields, descriptive text for food images, and layouts that adapt to different screen sizes. You can use your browser’s zoom controls to enlarge text.

Scope and limitations

We aim to make the website usable by as many people as possible. We have not completed an independent accessibility audit and do not claim full conformance with an accessibility standard. If a page or feature presents a barrier, please contact us for assistance.

Online ordering, maps, and social media open external services whose accessibility may differ. If an external ordering or booking experience is difficult to use, call the restaurant for help.

Visiting the restaurant

For questions about step-free access, seating, restrooms, or other accommodations, call before your visit so the team can discuss your needs. This page does not certify the physical accessibility of the premises.
