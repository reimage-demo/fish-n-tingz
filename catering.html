Catering questions

What is the minimum catering order?

Our catering menu has a minimum order serving 40–50 people. Call (860) 212-3349 to discuss your date, guest count, menu and arrangements.

What can I order for Jamaican catering?

Choose from snapper, oxtail, pepper steak, curry goat, whiting, fried chicken, curry chicken and jerk chicken, with rice and peas, white rice, cabbage or salad. Other items may be available upon request. Snapper and oxtail are market price; contact us for a current quote.

Can Fish N Tingz cater an event at Legacy Events CT?

Yes. You can request Fish N Tingz catering when booking Legacy Events CT, the separate venue downstairs. Venue rental and venue payments are handled by Legacy Events CT; catering is provided by Fish N Tingz.
