Before you visit

How do table reservation deposits work?

Parties of 10 or more require a deposit: $50 for 10 guests, plus $5 for each additional guest. No deposit is required for fewer than 10 guests. After your request is submitted, your receipt shows a reservation number and a Cash App link to $Fishntingz0. Include that number and your name in the payment note. Staff verify payment; sending a deposit does not automatically confirm a table. Every guest must be present within 30 minutes of the reservation time for the deposit to be refunded.

Is my table confirmed when I submit the form?

The form submits a request and gives you a reservation number. The team will contact you to confirm. Keep your number for questions, changes or matching a deposit. Table bookings have a one-hour minimum and a two-hour seating limit.

Who should I call about catering or venue rentals?

For Fish N Tingz catering, call (860) 212-3349. For Legacy Events CT venue rentals downstairs, call (860) 990-4505. Restaurant table reservations use (860) 730-4440.
