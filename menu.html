Before you visit

Can I order Fish N Tingz for pickup?

Use the Order on Toast link to place your order. Adding dishes to your website picks helps you plan; it does not place or pay for an order. Confirm final prices and availability during checkout.

Does Fish N Tingz serve Sunday brunch?

Yes. Our Sunday brunch menu includes dishes such as ackee and saltfish, chicken and waffles, and shrimp and grits. See brunch and event details, or call (860) 730-4440 for current availability.

How do I ask about allergens or dietary needs?

Call (860) 730-4440 or ask your server before ordering. Staff can discuss current ingredients and preparation. Menu descriptions and photographs do not establish that a dish is suitable for a particular allergy or diet.
